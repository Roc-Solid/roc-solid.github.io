The ecosystem · audio

A sound from a handler, in one line

Audio.play!("/sounds/eat.wav") plays a sound when someone clicks, presses a key or wins a game: any file the browser can play (WAV, MP3, Ogg), from your site or any URL, with nothing to load in advance. The showcase's games play their sounds this way.

Audio is the module you call. Both halves of an app compile it, so a component that plays a sound type-checks on the server too. AudioPlayer is a plugin that serves the player, a small script every page loads. An app that plays no sound doesn't list it, and its pages load no player.

When a sound plays#

A play is a Browser.call!, a call to the page's own JavaScript, and follows its rules:

  • It runs once the handler is done and the page shows what it wrote. A handler that waits for the server runs twice, but plays once.
  • Only a handler plays: an event's handler, a timer, or an effect's apply. Calling it while a hole, a memo or a component's body is computed is an error, and so is calling it on the server.
  • Browsers keep a page silent until the reader has clicked or typed on it. Until then nothing plays, and no error is reported.
  • A file that can't be played is reported on the browser's console. So is a play when the app doesn't list the plugin: "no effect is called audio.play: the audio plugin's script registers it: is the audio plugin in the app's plugins?"

How the player works#

The plugin serves its script at /rocx/plugins/audio/audio.js. Every page loads it before start.js, so it is ready before the page wakes. It registers the browser effect audio.play on window.rocxEffects, where Browser.call! looks. Each play makes an HTML Audio element, sets its volume and rate (kept between 0 and 1, and between 1/16 and 16), and plays it. The browser fetches a file on its first play, and caches it.

under, which the runtime gives every effect, puts a path starting with / under the site's base: /sounds/eat.wav becomes /rocx/sounds/eat.wav when your site is served at /rocx/.

On the Android host no script is needed: the phone's own handler for audio.play plays the file, fetched from your app's server, at the same volume and rate. The pomodoro example plays its chime this way.

Sound files#

The package brings no sounds. Your app serves its own, like any static file (--static). This site's are a few KB each, chiptune notes made by site/sounds.mjs; the quiz and pomodoro examples make theirs the same way.

Not yet#

  • A sound can't be stopped, paused or looped.
  • A play answers nothing: your code can't tell when a sound ends, or whether it played.
  • No loading ahead: the first play waits for the download.
